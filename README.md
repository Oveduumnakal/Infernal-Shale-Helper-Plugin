# Infernal Shale Helper

Infernal Shale Helper is a RuneLite plugin for mining infernal shale in the Chasm of Fire with Jim's wet cloth. It highlights what to click on each tick of the wipe rhythm, so you can keep the fast pace without counting ticks.

## Features

### The wipe rhythm

- **Cloth, then tile, then rock**

  Jim's wet cloth glows in your backpack when it is time to wipe. On the next tick the cloth stops glowing and the tile beside the closest shale rock lights up, so you know where to step. On the tick after that the rock itself lights up, so you know when to click it. Then the cloth glows again, ready for the next wipe.

- **Only live rocks**

  Rocks that were just mined are skipped until they grow back, so the tile always leads to a rock you can mine.

### Crushing

- **Chisel when it is time to crush**

  Once 9 infernal shale are in your backpack, the cloth stops glowing and your chisel or jeweller's chisel glows instead. It keeps glowing until every shale is crushed, then the cloth takes over again. The number is a setting.

### Settings

- **Your colors, your style**

  Pick the color for the cloth, tile, rock and chisel, and whether highlights are drawn as an outline, a fill, or both.
